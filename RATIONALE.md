# PageBender — Positioning Rationale

## Primary user
Small and independent publishers producing books in Indian languages, often with English mixed into the same manuscript. This is the lead audience because they repeatedly take manuscripts through page design and print preparation, and may have limited in-house production capacity.

## Problem to solve
The assignment brief identifies script-sensitive typesetting as a potential source of friction: conjuncts and matras may render incorrectly, and mixed-script pages can take time to lay out. These issues create manual review and correction work between manuscript completion and print-ready export.

## One-line value proposition
**PageBender is a considered book-typesetting workflow for publishers working across Indian scripts and English—from manuscript to print-ready pages.**

## Channel and message
**Primary channel: organic search.** The page is structured around the problem and audience language (Indian-language book typesetting, mixed-script publishing, print-ready pages). This is a reasoned channel choice, not a claim based on campaign performance. A secondary channel could be direct outreach to independent publishing houses and publishing communities.

## Alternative teardown: Adobe InDesign
InDesign is a credible professional page-layout alternative with extensive layout controls and a mature publishing workflow. It is flexible and familiar to many designers. The opportunity for PageBender is not to claim that InDesign cannot handle Indian scripts; that would be inaccurate without testing. Instead, PageBender can aim to make the manuscript-to-book workflow more focused and approachable for small publishers whose recurring work involves Indian scripts and mixed-script pages.

## Design choices
- Deep navy, blue, cyan and purple create a focused contemporary software identity.
- Space Grotesk display headings and DM Sans body text keep the page modern and readable.
- The hero shows a mixed Devanagari/English book spread immediately, making the use case tangible.
- The page follows a simple sequence: problem → workflow → audience → action.
- Assumption notes prevent concept copy from being mistaken for confirmed product specifications.
