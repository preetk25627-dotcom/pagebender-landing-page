# PageBender — Quick User Test

**Status: Pending.** Complete this with two real people. Do not invent participants or responses.

## Who to recruit
Prefer a small-publisher employee, independent author, freelance typesetter, book designer, editor or someone who has prepared a manuscript for print. Record their relationship to the target audience without collecting unnecessary personal information.

## Five-minute test
1. Show the landing page without explaining the product.
2. Ask: "What do you think PageBender does?"
3. Ask: "Who do you think this product is for?"
4. Ask: "What is confusing or unclear?"
5. Ask: "Would you consider trying it? Why or why not?"
6. Ask them to point to the section that most influenced their understanding.
